LOGO CONCEPT

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: primary symbol for Nest, an agentic workspace for Stellar DeFi research, comparison, and user-reviewed execution.
Primary request: Create one exceptionally simple, distinctive, flat geometric nest emblem. Three thick orange interwoven open elliptical U-shaped ribbons cradle one small round orange seed in the center; the result feels like a protective, ordered home for signals and capital. Nest bowl silhouette, balanced, compact, horizontally oval, few broad curves. Elegant precision, not an illustration of twigs. The outer two bands have gently staggered upward ends and an unmistakable open top. The seed is fully separated by generous negative space.
Style: clean vector-like solid one-color logo, crisp exact edges, no texture. Design reads clearly as a nest at small size, no Wi-Fi arcs. Built to later become extruded ribbons in a 3D scene.
Color: only vivid vermilion orange #ff5500 for the mark.
Composition: one large centered emblem, square canvas, generous transparent margins. Transparent background, no background shapes.
Text: none.
Avoid: birds, wings, feathers, houses, shields, stars, letters, gradients, shadows, glow, perspective, 3D, mockups, multiple variants, captions, watermarks.
