One thesis. Two actions.

Backtest and Set automation share one conversation. History keeps their original inputs.

Editable trigger presets

  • Pool arbitrage: compare XLM prices on Soroswap and Aquarius.
  • BTC momentum: a daily close crosses above the 20-day EMA; propose an XLM buy for review.
  • Volume breakout: XLM closes above its previous 30-day high with daily volume above twice its prior 20-day average.
  • Yield gap: an eligible USDC supply rate beats the current venue by two percentage points for 24 hours after fees, with sufficient exit and entry liquidity.

Presets fill the composer for editing. They never send, schedule or trade automatically. Signal drafts ask for their data source and preserve the original condition. They do not request arbitrage pools.

Conversation and review

The Strategy canvas, chart controls and separate settings panel are removed. The chat asks for missing source inputs, sizing when proposing a purchase, and the relevant lookback or monitoring window. A compact setup review appears only when those inputs are complete. Users refine it in chat before saving.

History supports search, type filters, inspection of the source and conversation, reuse, and removal with Undo. Old Lab routes resolve to the current Backtest or History view.

Source boundary

The design preview saves drafts on this device. Backtests await historical data; monitor setups are not scheduled. Source feeds and signal rules still require validation before evaluation or monitoring. No result, working data connection or financial execution is implied.
